## 1. Game Overview
- **Title:** Lighthouse
- **Genre:** Psychological Horror / Point-and-Click Narrative
- **Length:** 9 days, then one of the endings
- **Platform:** TBA
- **Target Audience:** TBA

## 2. Gameplay Mechanics

### Core Loop
Each day the keeper works through a task list: wash up, cook breakfast, record the weather, fix the lighthouse, empty the fish net, cook dinner, go to bed. Clinging to that routine is how he stays grounded, and the game breaks it a little more each day: tasks get added, crossed out and replaced as events take over. Each day opens with a dream sequence and has a journal entry.

### Sanity
- One hidden or visible **Sanity** score. Choices and events raise or lower it, and it shapes his perception and the ending.
- The latest writing doesn't assign numbers; values are set during balancing.
- Sanity sources named in the latest writing: fish net and flower keep/toss choices, scratching the wound, and horror events (corpses, the wall).

### Minigames
- **Fish net (every day):** items from the net come up one at a time in random order and the player chooses KEEP or DISCARD. The cycle repeats until the good fish is kept. Each day has its own catch pool (see [Day-by-Day](#4-day-by-day-walkthrough)). *Being workshopped:* keeping junk for dinner costs sanity.
- **Lighthouse maintenance:** fixing the lighthouse at the top floor. There's a loose brick on the staircase he has to put back every time he passes.
- **Flower picking (Day 5 on):** same format as the fish net, with flowers: KEEP good flowers, TOSS bugs and other junk.
- **Mermaid care (Day 4 on):** a menu of tools on the left, like the lighthouse maintenance. Each day is a sequence of steps, each with its own tool (gloves, alcohol, needle/fish bones, bandage, fish skin, flowers). The steps get worse each day as she rots.
- **Scratch pop-ups (Day 3 on):** "Scratch" prompts pop up all over the screen and only go away when clicked. They pile up the more they're ignored.

### Treasures
Small items left by past keepers and the drowned are hidden around the island, a few per day, each with a line of dialogue. On Day 8 they pay off: the corpses in the lighthouse walls are missing exactly these things (see [Treasure Catalogue](#6-treasure-catalogue)).

### Controls & UI
- Point-and-click interaction with scenes and objects.
- Task list (glows when updated) and a toolbar for tools/inventory.

## 3. Story and Setting

### Premise
You are a lighthouse keeper on an isolated island. On Day 1 your coworker Camborne is found dead. You keep up the routine because there's nothing else to do, but reality starts to shift. On Day 3 the lighthouse goes dark in a storm and a ship wrecks on the rocks: your fault. Among the drowned is a woman who seems to be breathing. You devote yourself to keeping her alive while she rots, and you neglect the lighthouse, which doesn't like being forgotten.

### Lore
- The lighthouse traps the souls of its keepers in its walls. A **red light** calls out to the souls trapped there (motif: a red light looming in the distance, reflected in bottles and goggles, red flashlights far out at sea).
- Each keeper it takes makes it taller: hidden photographs of past keepers show the lighthouse growing in each one.
- The walls are full of corpses, faces, flesh, teeth, hair and sea worms.

### Characters
- **The Keeper (MC):** the player character. Isolated, clinging to routine, unravelling.
- **Camborne:** the MC's coworker, dead from the first day. The opening cutscene is his life flashing before his eyes before he ends up inside the lighthouse wall. Found again on the other side of the stain on Day 8.
- **The Mermaid:** one of the shipwreck's drowned. She looks like a beautiful woman whose chest is moving, so the MC believes she's alive. Sea worms infest her and she rots day by day.
- **Past keepers:** known only through what they left behind and the photographs. They're in the walls.
- **Delivery person:** appears in the mermaid ending.

### Scenes
- **Bedroom:** the MC's bed, Camborne's bed (later the mermaid's), table with the journal, floorboards, window that shows the lighthouse at night.
- **Bathroom/Sink:** washing up, bandaging the hand.
- **Kitchen** and **Stove:** cooking fish and corn chowder.
- **Outside:** home, lighthouse exterior, path to the dock and the field.
- **Graveyard:** Camborne's grave, later the mass grave.
- **Dock:** the fish net, wreckage and floating corpses.
- **Field:** flower picking.
- **Lighthouse:** 1st floor, the staircase (with the "main stain" and the loose brick), top floor.
- **Mermaid care:** close-up of her on Camborne's bed.

## 4. Day-by-Day Walkthrough

### Day 1: Establishing the status quo
- **Opening cutscene:** Camborne's life flashes before his eyes: corpses and people brutalized on a battlefield, being underwater, a red light above, drinking from a bottle, black. Sounds of the lighthouse creaking and spinning. Then stone cracking and crumbling until there's a gap to peek out of: a stone wall and stairs.
- **Journal:** normal wildlife.
- **Routine:** wash up, cook breakfast, fix the lighthouse, record the weather, empty the fish net. He passes the **main stain** on the staircase each time he climbs.
- **Treasures:** Pen (graveyard), Oversized boot (lighthouse exterior), Golden tooth (dock). Hints of past keepers and stains/cracks around the environment, some of which change shape later.
- **Fish net:** seaweed, good fish, coral, pebbles.
- **Camborne:** he chops up the fish for dinner, then tries to wake Camborne and realizes he's been dead for hours. A brief mental breakdown, then he takes the body outside.

### Day 2: Reality starts to shift
- **Dream (1→2):** Camborne in the water.
- He's alone now but sticks to the routine.
- **Breakfast:** while he chops the fish, it turns to look at him. He doesn't know if it's real.
- **Lighthouse:** a stain or crack seems to have changed places. The loose brick has to be put back, which happens every day, so he doesn't even think about it anymore.
- **Fish net:** there might be a large shadow in the water. Catch: seaweed, coral, good fish, sea glass, can.
- **Treasures:** Wallet (under Camborne's mattress), Bifocal glasses (top of the lighthouse).
- **The grave:** before he can go in for dinner, he finds the storm last night has washed Camborne's grave away. Reburying him, he sees Camborne's face has caved in and is completely gone, though the rest of the body is rotting at a normal rate. He injures his hand.
- **Bandage hand:** at the bathroom sink, before dinner.
- **Dinner:** he refuses to use fish; the thought of flesh makes him sick.
- **End of day:** show a very stormy night.

### Day 3: The storm
- **Dream (2→3):** the storm.
- **Journal:** maggots and corn.
- **Wound:** festering and itching like crazy. Scratch pop-ups start (scratch or resist).
- **Breakfast:** he can't swallow anything but corn chowder.
- **Outside:** sweltering hot, not a cloud in sight. The routine goes on with a few treasures, and even the stains are back in their original spots, as if everything were back to normal.
- **Treasures:** Ace of Spades (bedroom floorboards), Flask (lighthouse staircase near the fallen brick; it's Camborne's).
- **Fish net:** a single sea worm, good fish, coral, bottle, can.
- **Weather:** he marks it as sunny, then gets a strange feeling and second-guesses himself ("Are you sure?"). He blinks and it's thunderstorming like crazy and he's freezing. The lighthouse isn't on, a huge danger to ships.
- **Lighthouse:** he rushes to fix it but the scissors are missing. Searching in a panic, he finds them stuck between two bricks on the staircase, as if something in the walls stole them and was dragging them in. He grabs them and gets the lighthouse working.
- **Dinner**, then he collapses into sleep.

### Day 4: The shipwreck
- **Dream (3→4):** working on the lighthouse, the scissors are missing again. Downstairs he finds them in a larger gap in the wall; reaching for them, they're dragged into the dark. He reaches in, hand then whole arm, to squelching flesh sounds that shouldn't be there. He gets the scissors out, his hand covered in sea worms and rotting flesh. Looking into the hole again: breathing, groaning, rotted flesh moving. Jumpscare: a fleshy rotted hand reaches out of the wall and grabs his arm.
- **Wake:** flies and maggots in the festering wound (same hand). He washes it at the sink a few extra times. The scratch prompts keep tormenting him.
- **After breakfast:** outside, a giant shipwreck. It's his fault they all died.
- **Scraps:** he picks up treasures and scraps at the docks, including a different brand of corn chowder. **Treasure:** Lighter (lighthouse 1st floor).
- **Fish net:** good fish, single sea worm, seaweed, coral, plus a Pocket watch and a Crucifix (once each).
- **The dead:** floating corpses at the dock, one of them a rotting bride ("Died on her wedding day"). He carries them one by one to a mass grave.
- **The woman:** going back one more time, there's one corpse not like the others: a beautiful woman whose chest is moving up and down. She looks alive. He takes her inside to Camborne's bed. The "Fix the lighthouse" task is crossed out; there are more important things now.
- **Mermaid care:**
  1. Remove barnacles/seaweed (click each; gloves)
  2. Disinfect the wound (alcohol)
  3. Suture the wound (needle)
  4. Wrap the wound (bandage)
  5. Feed her (bowl of chowder)
- **Night:** he gave her all the food, so he eats no dinner and collapses after tending to her all night.

### Day 5: She's waiting for me
- **Dream (4→5):** the mermaid sleeping peacefully next to him; jumpscare of her as a nasty corpse.
- **Wake:** in a panic, unreasonably anxious that she'll rot away like in the dream. Worms are coming out of her legs.
- **Mermaid care:**
  1. Splat worms (no tool needed)
  2. Unwrap the pus- and blood-filled bandages to assess the damage
  3. Disinfect and clean the wound (wipe the yellow pus off with alcohol)
  4. Peel fish skin ×3 and graft #1 (stomach), over the gnarlier wounds
  5. Stitch it
  6. Rewrap (smaller wounds)
- **Outside:** "Fix the lighthouse" is crossed out and replaced with **"Pick flowers for her"** (flower minigame). The fish net task is now **"Collect fish for her."**
- **Treasures:** Bony ring finger with ring (field: "The ring could fetch some money."), Medium-sized lid (lighthouse staircase: "Has this always been here?").
- **Fish net:** rotten seaweed, good fish, coral, sea glass, a bunch of sea worms.
- **Lighthouse:** every time he goes to the lighthouse door: "She's waiting for me."

### Day 6
- **Mermaid care:**
  1. Splat worms (gloves)
  2. Unwrap the wound
  3. Disinfect the wound (alcohol)
  4. Peel fish skin and graft open gash #2 (legs) (fish)
  5. Stitch it up
  6. Rebandage the rest of the wound
  7. Cover the smell (9 red poppies)
- **Outside:** flower minigame, fish net, ignore the lighthouse.
- **Night:** the lighthouse flashes through the bedroom window; he thinks it's just lightning.

### Day 7
- **Outside:** flower minigame, fish net, ignore the lighthouse.
- **Mermaid care:**
  1. Splat even more worms (gloves)
  2. Rip off the rotted flowers tangled around her (gloves)
  3. Take off the bandages (gloves)
  4. Disinfect the wound (alcohol)
  5. Graft open gash #3 (arms and neck) (fish)
  6. Cover the smell (red poppies)

### Day 8: The lighthouse
- **Dream:** continues the previous dream. It's night and she's gone from her bed. He looks around the dark room and sees her wormy hand reach up from under his bed. She slowly peeks up, face in shadow, then jumpscare: red light pours out of her eyes.
- **Wake:** in a cold sweat. Goes out to pick flowers.
- **Fish net:** rotten fish, a bunch of sea worms, seaweed, rotten seaweed, can, good fish.
- **The lighthouse groans:** after the flowers, a loud creaking, breaking sound from the lighthouse. It must be breaking down because he neglected it.
- **Her legs** have burst into worms (2 frames). Dialogue about the stench.
- **Mermaid care:**
  1. Splat two massive worms (gloves)
  2. Rip off the wilted red poppies, which have torn her skin (gloves)
  3. Disinfect the wound (alcohol)
  4. Graft open gash #4 (chest) (fish)
  5. Stitch her legs together
  6. Cover the smell (red poppies + white daisies)
- **"I haven't checked the lighthouse in 2 days. I'm in big trouble."** The care task is crossed out and replaced with the lighthouse. The lighthouse is angry at being forgotten.
- **The climb:** the lighthouse is taller now and more ominous. The climb takes longer; he puts the loose brick back as usual, and it takes two extra flights to reach the top ("I need to catch my breath"). He fixes the lighthouse.
- **The descent:** the stairs get darker and never end, and the back button disappears. He passes the familiar loose brick again, as if looping. Bricks fall out, revealing worms, flesh and corpses that allude to the treasures he found. He loops back to the loose brick, and on the other side of the stain he finds **Camborne**. In terror he runs down for an unspecified time until he's outside.
- **"I'm not going back in there. I just need to take care of her."** From now on, clicking the lighthouse: "I'm not going back in there."

### Day 9: The date
- **Dream:** TBD. **Journal:** TBD.
- He completely rejects the lighthouse and focuses on the mermaid to cope.
- **Flower minigame.**
- **Fish net:** rotten seaweed, rotten fish, sea worms, rotten bottle with worms (2 frames), good fish.
- **Mermaid care:**
  1. Splat worms
  2. Replace the flowers (red poppy)
  3. Remove the stitches
  4. Dress her up (fish net)
- **The dock date:** he thinks fresh air will do her good and carries her down to the dock, past the lighthouse. 25 seconds of romantic dock date. The screen slowly fades to black and he falls asleep.
- **High tide:** he wakes at night. She's gone and the water is up to his waist. He finds her floating a little way out and fishes her out of the water.
- **Choice:**
  - "Oh, she's gone fr" → bury her
  - "No, she's ok. She just needs more rest" → take her back to bed

## 5. Endings

**Insanity / Mermaid Ending**
She's awake and staring at you. Clicking her, she nuzzles your hand. The usual mermaid chores, but now she looks like an ethereal mermaid. Take her downstairs and make her breakfast. A knock: a delivery person comes onto the dock, takes one look at you, screams and runs away.

**Lighthouse Ending**
The MC fixes the lighthouse one last time. On the way down the loose brick has fallen out again, and the hole opens up bigger. Clicking it, he crawls inside to join the other dead. A brief vision of all the past keepers, then a flash of him stuck in the wall screaming in agony like the rest of them, and the lighthouse growing taller. Flashing images, cut to black.

**No Mermaid**
The next morning Camborne's sheets are stained with rot and worms. He takes them out and buries them along with all the trinkets he collected, then goes through the Day 1 tasks with nothing strange happening anymore.

## 8. Open Questions

## 9. Stretch Goals (Being Workshopped)
- **The rules:** a list of keeper rules hidden somewhere for observant players (beer bottle, soup pot, the beach): two workers must always be present; fix the lighthouse every day; ration food; don't leave meat out more than 5 hours; remember exactly where you put everything; don't remove the bricks; never stare into the ocean at night. On later days the rules subtly change (5 hours → 3; "never look into the ocean at night" → "Look into the ocean"), with a "Did one of the rules change?" quiz where sometimes nothing changed.
- Sea worms scooped up in the net after the shipwreck.
- Camborne's leg appearing randomly (something under the table?).
- Looking for constellations in a pitch-black sky, which keeps the player at the dock staring into the sea.
